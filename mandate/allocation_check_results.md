# Allocation check: results

The equity series is Kenneth French's market return for five regions, at the equity ETF's weights by region in its holdings file of 2026-11-13, the same weights in every month, converted to euro at the ECB's month-end rate: North America 67.9 per cent, Europe 13.9 per cent, Japan 5.0 per cent, Asia Pacific ex Japan 2.1 per cent, Emerging markets 11.1 per cent. Each country goes to the region of French's lists that holds it (method/french_regions.csv), and Israel, 0.23 per cent of the fund, Russia, 0.00 per cent of the fund, Cash and derivatives, 0.50 per cent of the fund, are in none of them, so the five weights are scaled to sum to one. The series answers what French's regional markets, at the weights by region the fund had on that date, would have lost over the test's months, and not what the index lost with the weights it had then. The bonds are a zero-coupon bond of 7 years priced from the ECB's euro area curve.

A split is the pair of target weights, written equity/bonds. Each split starts at its target weights and is rebalanced by its band, the interval of 5 points either side of the equity target weight: when the equity weight leaves it at a month end, the portfolio goes back to the target weights. The worst fall is the largest fall in value from a previous peak to a later low, and a fall in a named period runs from the highest earlier value to the lowest month end in that period. Growth per year is the compound annual return, and volatility is the standard deviation of the monthly returns times the square root of 12.
